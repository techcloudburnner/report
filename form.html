Bank Statements

Key Points About Bank Statements

  • Bank statements provide a summary of all transactions in a specific period, including deposits, withdrawals, and fees.
  • They are useful for tracking expenses, budgeting, and identifying unauthorized transactions.
  • Statements often include account details like account number, branch code, and contact information for the bank.
  • You can use bank statements for tax filing, loan applications, or financial audits.
  • Ensure to review statements regularly to maintain financial accuracy and security.
